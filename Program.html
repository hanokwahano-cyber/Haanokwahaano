Our Programs

OUR APPROACH

Working Together for Positive Change

Hano Kwa Hano Organization focuses on programs that respond to the needs of refugees, vulnerable people and communities. We work with communities and partners to identify needs and develop practical responses.

Our programs are designed to promote dignity, opportunity, participation and long-term community development.
