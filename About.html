WHO WE ARE

About Hano Kwa Hano

The name of the C.B.O is “ Hano Kwa Hano”. This name has been derived from the ethnic group of Somali Bantu, who were a minority in Somalia, descended from South Eastern Africa as slaves in the 19th century, and who concentrated in the Juba and Shabelle River valleys. Their origins are from Mozambique, Malawi, and Tanzania. The name “HANO KWA HANO” means “JUST HERE whereby you can identify your potential dream from the place you are dwelling. You are not limited to the environment. So the target of the C.B.O is to bring awareness in the community; for example, the status of being a refugee is not a reason for being vulnerable and having a miserable life. Thus the C.B.O is oriented to uplift the young upcoming generation to be self reliance and cope up with challenges in life without depending an external back up. Together we work to support people, strengthen communities and create opportunities for a better future.

ABOUT OUR ORGANIZATION

Serving People and Strengthening Communities

Hano Kwa Hano Organization is a community-based organization committed to supporting refugees, vulnerable people and communities facing social and economic challenges.

We believe that every person has dignity, potential and the right to opportunities that can help them build a better future.

Through community-based initiatives, partnerships and practical support, we seek to respond to important needs while encouraging people to participate in solutions that affect their lives.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission

👁

Our Vision

A society where refugees and vulnerable people live with dignity, have access to opportunities and are empowered to build safe, sustainable and hopeful futures.

🎯

Our Mission

The purpose of this group is to identify the talents which are not observed due to lack of awareness and starting gear to bring out what is inside the young generation in the community. The C.B.O is targeting both the refugees and the host to be self reliance at their place they are living from age 18-45 old.
