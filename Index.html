WHO WE ARE

About Hano Kwa Hano

Serving People, Strengthening Communities

Hano Kwa Hano Organization is a community-based organization committed to supporting refugees, vulnerable people and communities in need.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, opportunity, education and access to basic needs. Through community-focused programs, we work together with individuals, partners and local communities to create meaningful and sustainable change.
